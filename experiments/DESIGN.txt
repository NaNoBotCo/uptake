UPTAKE CADENCE TRIAL — design and state
=======================================
Assigned 19 Sept 2026. Window 22 Sept – 3 Oct.

THE QUESTION
------------
Fourteen days of the fleet's own traffic say a repo-day with a commit draws a
median of 12 unique cloners and a repo-day without one draws 0. Excluding 18
Sept, when 49 repos were pushed at once, it is 6.6 against 0.4 — still sixteen
times. That a push draws machines is therefore not in question here.

What is in question: does the push have to carry anything? If a timestamp bump
pulls the same machines as a real change, the finding is about the event and
not the work. If it does not, the event is only a notification and the content
is what earns the copy.

THE ARMS
--------
Cadence is held constant. The single variable is whether the push carries real
content. Both arms fire on the same five days: 22, 25, 28 Sept, 1, 3 Oct.

  arm1_noop    7 repos   a probe file's timestamp changes, nothing else
  arm2_real    7 repos   a genuine content change, prepared in advance
  holdout      7 repos   untouched for the duration
  benched      8 repos   left quiet, not analysed

Arm 2 was picked on whether a repo has real work to ship five times, which makes
it a chosen set rather than a random one. Left uncorrected that is the whole
result: repos with live data behind them are also the repos a crawler may favour
for reasons that have nothing to do with cadence. So arm 1 was cut from fifteen
to seven, each one the nearest match to an arm 2 repo by how many machines it
drew on 18 Sept. Five of the seven pairs match within one machine.

  uptake             43   st-expedite-wiki   36
  flighthelper       32   wichaa-vault       32
  visa-desk          29   thairoots          28
  amulet-market-api  27   pdf-tool           27
  mueang-map         24   kali-yantra        24
  cm-womens-health   15   offramp            14
  skipdjt            12   hak-farang         12
                   26.0 mean               24.7

Four of arm 2 can sustain five real pushes because something live sits behind
them: uptake regenerates its corpus and this trial's own data, mueang-map pulls
province boundaries, cm-womens-health fetches from OSM, amulet-market-api
rebuilds its snapshot. Three carry one real fix each and will push fewer times —
flighthelper's PBI to DJT code flip of 18 August, which stale caches still emit,
visa-desk's work already in flight, and skipdjt's own build output stranded in a
stash. Seven repos were dropped from arm 2 for having no live source, no named
open item and nothing in flight: neurovizr-decoded, homematch, stocktake,
swanbot, blinking-twelve, lanna-almanac, baanstaff.

Because the arms will not fire the same number of times, the unit of analysis is
the push and not the repo: machines drawn in the 24 hours after each push,
keyed to the commit ledger, so a push that did not happen cannot be counted as
one that drew nothing.

WHAT IS EXCLUDED, AND WHY
-------------------------
19 repos had commits on two or more days in the baseline window and are under
active development; freezing them would cost real work. index is excluded
separately — haus.index.nightly pushes it real content at 04:10 daily, so it
cannot be a no-op repo. Repos with no remote are out of reach by construction.

THE SEQUESTER
-------------
The holdout is guarded twice. push_walk.py skips it by name, and each of the
seven has a pre-push hook. Both expire by themselves on 3 Oct rather than
waiting to be remembered. Commits still work; only the push is held. A
deliberate override is git push --no-verify.

LIMITS
------
A clone is not a read. The traffic endpoints carry no user agent, so nothing
here identifies an operator or shows that anything was ingested.

Anonymous cloners are counted by IP and window uniques are not the sum of the
dailies. Per-repo uniques are never deduplicated across repos.

Arm 2 depends on real work existing to ship. On 19 Sept only 3 of its 14 repos
had any, which is why the start moved to the 22nd. Any repo with nothing real
to ship on a push day is skipped and logged, not filled with a token change.
